BUILT FOR THE PHYSICS

Not a sampler.
A motor workstation.

01

Physical pitch mapping

Resolve pitch into blade passing, commutation, or step pulses, then map it to RPM and duty cycle.

02

Professional piano roll

Drag, marquee-select, resize, zoom, and undo while range, timing, and concurrency warnings stay visible.

03

Multi-motor acoustics

Every voice has its own inertia, noise, placement, and timbre. Hear the result before building the rig.

04

MIDI arranging

Extract melody and bass layers, then resample around each motor's settling time to create a fan-playable score.

WORKFLOW

Start with one melody.

  1. Import MIDI or edit .fscoreChoose voice count, duration, and BPM.
  2. Calibrate in the rollCatch range limits, slow settling, and low-frequency masking.
  3. Render and auditionGenerate a stereo preview with airflow noise, pan, and room reverb.
  4. Connect hardwareDrive motors over Arduino or ESP32 with the planned duty timeline.
